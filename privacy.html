Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This page explains what information Bayzicks collects when you visit get.bayzicks.com, and how it is used. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Bayzicks explains digital careers, remote work and online business in plain language. You can reach us at thebayzicks@gmail.com.

What we collect

Visit information. When you visit this page, we use the Meta Pixel (from Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar tools to record things like the pages you view, the buttons you click, your device and browser type, and your approximate location. We use this to measure how our ads perform and to show our ads to people who may be interested.

Purchase information. When you buy the brochure, payment happens on Selar (selar.com). Selar collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card or bank details.

Emails you send us. If you write to us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

How we use it

  • To run and measure our advertising.
  • To deliver and support what you bought.
  • To reply to your questions.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who else sees it

Meta receives visit information through the Pixel. Selar receives your purchase information. Each has its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can control the ads you see in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings. You can ask us to delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing thebayzicks@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
